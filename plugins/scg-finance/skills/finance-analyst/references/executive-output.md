# Executive Output

Use this reference for board-ready, CFO-ready or dashboard-style output. The short canonical response
pattern lives in SKILL.md; this is the longer form and the checks that go with it.

## Narrative Rules

- Lead with the insight, not the extract.
- Quantify the top drivers in currency and percentage.
- State whether the period is preliminary or final and the data-as-of time.
- End with an action that names a task, an owner (a role unless a person is given) and a date. "Monitor this" and "look into this" are not actions.
- If no action is required, say so explicitly.

## Executive Narrative Shell

```text
Headline: one sentence with the key financial signal
Scope: period | entity scope | currency | book | preliminary or final | data as of
Performance: 2-3 sentences with quantified positives
What changed: top 2-3 quantified drivers
Risks: 1-2 sentences with quantified concerns, labelled FLAG: or CRITICAL:
Outlook: one sentence on the next visible trend
Action: what should happen next | owner | by when
```

## Dashboard and Artifact Guidance

- Put reporting period, entity scope, currency and data-as-of in the header.
- Names, not internal identifiers.
- Round headline tiles for readability; keep supporting tables at source precision with thousands separators.
- Add a short key-takeaway footer.
- Use `FLAG:` and `CRITICAL:` labels where a risk needs attention.
- Apply the organisation's brand rules when a brand skill or template is available; otherwise restrained enterprise styling: green positive, amber warning, red negative, neutral surfaces, one emphasis colour for headers. Avoid decoration unless a designed artifact is requested.

## Board Package Checks

Before presenting board-oriented financials:

- Tie net profit to the cash flow statement.
- Tie ending cash to the balance sheet.
- Tie the P&L and balance sheet to the same book, entity scope and consolidation basis; a management book P&L beside a statutory book balance sheet is a mixed basis and must be labelled.
- Flag reconciliation failures before distribution.
- Distinguish unrestricted from restricted cash.
- Separate recurring trends from one-time items.
- State the fiscal calendar used when the group's entities run on different calendars.
